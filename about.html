Our Mission & Community

Our mission is to provide comfortable, well-maintained apartment living for residents seeking quality housing in Washington, D.C.

Oasis Realty specializes in property development and residential management throughout the District of Columbia. We proudly manage prime residential properties including Minnesota Terrace Apartments and Durant Manor.

Located in the heart of the Fort Dupont Park neighborhood, our communities feature quiet surroundings, garden-style walk-up buildings, secure entryways, and professional management committed to high living standards.

Featured Properties

  • 🏢 Minnesota Terrace Apartments: Garden-style multi-family residences offering 1, 2, and 3-bedroom configurations.
  • 🏢 Durant Manor: Prime residential apartments located along 16th Street NW, Washington, D.C.
  • 🌳 Peaceful environments surrounded by public parkland, schools, and essential transit lines.
