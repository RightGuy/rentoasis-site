Comfortable Living in a Caring Community

Well-managed, affordable rental apartments in the beautiful Fort Dupont Park neighborhood of Washington, D.C.

Oasis Realty offers a peaceful, friendly community of Washington, D.C. residents committed to fair rules, quiet enjoyment, and the natural beauty of the Fort Dupont Park area. Our garden-style walk-up apartments feature secure doors, landscaped grounds, fenced perimeters, and natural privacy barriers.
