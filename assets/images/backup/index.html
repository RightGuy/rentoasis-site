Welcome

Well Managed and Affordable Rental Apartments are sometimes difficult to find unless you first come and see "Minnesota Terrace Apartments". Here you will find a caring community of Washington D.C. residents that share a commitment to follow our fair rules and enjoy the relative peace and beauty and other benefits of the Fort Dupont Park neighborhood of our Nation's Capitol.
Conveniently located near Golfing, Shopping, and Entertainment and Sports Centers and Medical Facilities. We have plenty of Off-Street Parking and MetroBus and MetroRail Subway routes are right nearby for your easy travel needs. Lovely Fort Dupont Park is only a short walk or bike ride away.
"Garden Style" walk-up apartment living with secure doors and well landscaped grounds that add beauty to our buildings which are fenced and also guarded by natural barriers for your privacy.
